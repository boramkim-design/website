About Me

Educator turned Designer

To me, designing a learning experience and designing a product share the same core DNA. Both start with deep empathy, understanding needs, analyzing insights, and iterating through testing and feedback. Effective collaboration and communication drive both fields.

Building on this foundation, product design excites me because it allows me to apply my curiosity, analytical thinking, and love for technology on a broader scale. While I’ve always found deep purpose in helping others, design enables me to make a positive impact for an even wider audience by building thoughtful products and intuitive systems.

Outside of work, I balance my focus with relaxing jazz or chill reggae, love connecting with great people, and recharge by spending peaceful time in the mountains. I’m always eager to explore what I can build next, create meaningful experiences, and craft products that genuinely help people.
