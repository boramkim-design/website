About Me

How I got here

Before I designed interfaces, I designed lessons. Ten years in classrooms across two countries taught me that good design and good teaching solve the same problem: making something complicated feel obvious.

That instinct followed me into research interviews with patients, into a conference program read across time zones, into products used by people who will never meet me. Every audience is different, but the job is the same: listen closely, then build the clearest possible path from confusion to understanding.

These days I design for people who are often the last to be designed for: rural communities, Indigenous partners, patients, students aged 2 to 50. I care less about a screen looking clever and more about whether the person on the other side of it actually gets what they came for.
